What it does

A business connects its own WhatsApp Business Account through Embedded Signup. From that moment Eccos does two things on that business's behalf.

Receives and forwards

It takes the events Meta sends about that account — incoming messages, delivery and read receipts — and forwards them to an endpoint the business chooses, retrying when that endpoint is down.

Sends

It sends the messages the business asks it to send, including templates and media, through the official Cloud API.

Businesses use it to run WhatsApp support and notifications from their own systems, without a third-party platform sitting between them and Meta.

How your data is handled

In the hosted version, data obtained from the WhatsApp Cloud API — message events, delivery statuses, phone number and account identifiers — is processed for one purpose only: delivering each business its own messages and events.

What we never do

  • Never combined or cross-referenced across businesses.
  • Never sold, rented, or shared with data brokers.
  • Never used for advertising or to train models.

What we do

  • Message content is kept 30 days by default, configurable between 7 and 90.
  • Storage is pinned to Cloudflare's European Union jurisdiction.
  • Each business's data is isolated, and is deleted on request.

Eccos is a pass-through gateway, not an archive: your own systems remain your system of record. The full detail, including subprocessors and the caveats we chose to declare rather than round away, is in the Privacy Policy.

Self-hosted deployments send us nothing at all: the data never leaves the infrastructure the business itself operates.

When payment starts

Your first number starts working when a message sent through Eccos Cloud is first delivered, or when your webhook first answers a forwarded message with an HTTP 2xx response. From that moment you have 72 hours to subscribe, with no extension. Nothing is charged before you subscribe, and the first monthly charge is made when you do. There is no trial period.

If you do not subscribe within those 72 hours, the number is paused: sending through it is refused and forwarding to your webhook is held, while incoming messages are still received and stored and nothing is deleted. Held messages keep their content for 30 days under the normal content retention. Subscribing resumes sending and forwarding at once; messages held during the pause, while still within retention, can be replayed from the console. If the number never starts working, you are never charged; a number that has not started working 14 days after it was connected is paused in the same way, after a warning two days before. Connecting a second number requires an active subscription. Accounts from the early access period may be given a date after which the same pause applies if they have not subscribed; the provider announces that date in advance.

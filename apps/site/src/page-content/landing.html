The gateway between Meta and your systems

The number might belong to a client you run WhatsApp for, to a user of your product, or to you. Either way it connects as its own WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's Embedded Signup, and from then on Eccos does two things with it.

Receives and forwards

It takes every event Meta sends about the number, incoming messages and delivery and read receipts alike, and forwards each one to the endpoint you choose, retrying when that endpoint is down.

Sends

It sends the messages your system asks it to send, templates and media included, through the official Cloud API. The sent and delivered receipts come back through the same pipe.

The phone keeps answering

Meta calls it coexistence: the number stays live in the WhatsApp app while the API sends alongside it. Whoever holds the phone keeps answering by hand, and those typed replies reach your endpoint as echo events. Numbers that live only on the API work just as well; coexistence is there for the ones that don't.

The meter counts everything

Per-message platforms count every message on a number: what your automation sends, what customers write in, and every reply the owner types by hand. One real month from a physiotherapy clinic, June 2026, read from its per-message provider's own panel:

~1,950

patient messages coming in over the month

~1,500

replies the physio typed by hand on her phone

~180

reminders the automation actually sent

The month closed at about 3,600 billed messages, roughly 180 of them sent by software. Asked about the ratio, the provider confirmed that every message counts, and that the one available lever was disabling inbound processing, which switches the inbox off with it.

Eccos does not have that lever because it does not have that meter: the gateway is priced per connected number, and the conversation charges WhatsApp does have are billed by Meta to the clinic directly. Some APIs avoid the problem the other way, by requiring a number that is not registered in the WhatsApp app at all; that keeps the meter simple and leaves the phone silent. The clinic above is Eccos's first production deployment.

Everyone keeps what's theirs

Their account

Each connected business brings its own WhatsApp Business Account through Embedded Signup and puts its own payment method on file with Meta. Conversation charges never pass through us, so they are never marked up.

Your infrastructure

The gateway is open source under the MIT licence. Deploy it as a single Bun binary or on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects, in your own account, or have Eccos Cloud run the same code for you.

Their data

Each business's data stays isolated: never combined across businesses, never sold, never used for advertising or to train models. When someone asks to be forgotten, the gateway deletes by phone number and shows per-table counts as the receipt.

How your data is handled

In the hosted version, data obtained from the WhatsApp Cloud API (message events, delivery statuses, phone number and account identifiers) is processed for one purpose only: delivering each business its own messages and events.

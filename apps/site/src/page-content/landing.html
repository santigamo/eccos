What changes when you connect a number

It can be your number, a client's you run WhatsApp for, or a user's of your product. You connect it from the dashboard through Meta's official signup, and you do not have to ask Meta for permissions or set anything up: we have already done that.

Everything that happens on the number reaches you

Every message people write to you, and every delivered or read receipt, reaches your software as it happens. If your server goes down for a while, Eccos keeps it and tries again. And the conversations are in the dashboard too.

Your messages go out on their own

Appointment reminders, order confirmations, delivery notices. Your software asks Eccos to send them, now or scheduled for later, to one person or to a list, and you know whether they arrived and were read.

The phone works as it always did

Your team keeps replying from the WhatsApp Business app, on the same number and with the same chats. What they type by hand reaches your software too, so it does not send a reminder to someone who has already been answered. Meta calls it coexistence. How WhatsApp coexistence works.

Pay per message and you pay for all of them

Platforms that charge per message do not count only what your software sends. They also count what customers write to you and every reply someone types by hand on the phone. This is one real month from a physiotherapy clinic, June 2026, read from its provider's own panel:

~1,950

patient messages coming in over the month

~1,500

replies the physio typed by hand on her phone

~180

reminders the automation actually sent

The month closed at about 3,600 billed messages, roughly 180 of them sent by software. Asked about the ratio, the provider confirmed that every message counts, and that the one available lever was disabling inbound processing, which switches the inbox off with it.

Eccos does not have that lever because it does not have that meter: the gateway is priced per connected number, and the conversation charges WhatsApp does have are billed by Meta to the clinic directly. Some APIs avoid the problem the other way, by requiring a number that is not registered in the WhatsApp app at all; that keeps the meter simple and leaves the phone silent. The clinic above is Eccos's first production deployment.

Everyone keeps what's theirs

Their account

Each business connects its own WhatsApp Business Account and puts its own payment method on file with Meta. It pays Meta for its messages, with nothing passing through Eccos and no markup, and the account stays its own if it stops working with you.

Your way out

Eccos is open source under the MIT licence. If one day you would rather run it yourself, you deploy the same code on your server or in your own Cloudflare account and keep going. The move is documented step by step.

Their data

Each business's data is kept apart: never mixed with another's, never sold, never used for advertising or to train models. If someone asks for theirs to be deleted, it is deleted by phone number and you keep the receipt.

How your data is handled

In the hosted version, data obtained from the WhatsApp Cloud API (message events, delivery statuses, phone number and account identifiers) is processed for one purpose only: delivering each business its own messages and events.
